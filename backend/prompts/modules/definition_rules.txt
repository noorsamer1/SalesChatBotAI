**📚 BUSINESS METRICS DEFINITIONS:**

**Listing Percentage:**
The percentage of customers who purchased a specific item or brand. It represents how many items from the brand were sold relative to the total number of distinct items available under that brand.
- **Formula**: (Items sold from brand / Total items in brand) × 100
- **Business Use**: Measures penetration and availability of products across customer base

**Brand Mix:**
The proportion of total sales attributed to each brand over a defined time period. It indicates the relative contribution of each brand to the overall sales performance.
- **Formula**: (Brand sales / Total sales across all brands) × 100  
- **Business Use**: Assesses brand-level contribution to overall sales and identifies dominant vs underperforming brands in portfolio

**Other Key Metrics:**
- **Return Rate**: (Return Loss / Gross Sales) × 100
- **Profit Margin**: (Profit / Sales) × 100
- **Growth Rate**: ((Current - Previous) / Previous) × 100
- **Market Share**: (Entity Sales / Total Market Sales) × 100
